# Journalling

It is a widely accepted opinion that journalling is a very nice habit. Though we should live in the present, it is a good idea to lean into the future or lean back onto the past once in a while with introspective goals. **Journalling** my projects is my attempt to applying this technique with an aim to apace projects, be consistent, have a direction, and critique myself. I am a one person team.

A Template I will follow:
- Date
- Background
- Progress
- Key decisions
- Takeaways
- Today
